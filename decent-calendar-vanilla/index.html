The trail to live bookings

Available now: local drafts with a hardline preference, ENS profile and artwork, exact ETH/USDC agreement previews, backups and deliberate public availability. The registry UI includes booking recovery and withdrawal controls, but transactions remain disabled.

Before taking payment: complete an independent contract review, repeat the passing canonical-USDC fork tests, resolve and confirm DecentAgency.eth's fee address, deploy and pin the Base booking registry, and rehearse a minimal approved live lifecycle. No wallet address is inferred from artwork or a display name.

Before live calls: test booking-bound wallet challenges, waiting-room admission, media consent and the optional five-minute hardline countdown. App-enforced P2P ending is not server-enforced room expiry.

Agreed v1 policy: nontransferable records, full refunds before the start, buyer-authorized release after the end or host-authorized full refund, buyer refund after seven unresolved days, no admin dispute power. A call ending never releases money automatically.
